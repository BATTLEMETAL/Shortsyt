Privacy Policy — Shortsyt

Effective date: October 20, 2026

Summary: Shortsyt does not collect any personal data. All data stays on your device.

1. Overview

Shortsyt is a personal automation tool for publishing gaming highlight clips. This policy explains what data is handled and how.

2. Data We Collect

Shortsyt does not collect any personal data. Specifically:

  • No user account data is stored on external servers
  • No analytics or tracking is performed
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties
  • No cookies are used

3. OAuth Tokens (TikTok API)

When you authorize Shortsyt to access your TikTok account:

  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on your device only
  • Only video.publish scope is requested — for uploading videos on your behalf
  • Only an anonymous open_id identifier is stored locally — no profile name, email, or personal information is accessed
  • Tokens can be revoked at any time via TikTok Settings → Security → Manage app permissions

4. Video Content

  • Gameplay footage is processed locally on your device using FFmpeg
  • Rendered clips are uploaded directly to your authorized TikTok account
  • No video content passes through Shortsyt servers (there are none)

5. TikTok API Compliance

Shortsyt uses TikTok's Content Posting API solely to publish video content. We access only the minimum required data and do not store, share, or use TikTok user data beyond what is necessary for the upload operation.

6. Your Rights

You may revoke access at any time:

  • TikTok: Settings & Privacy → Security → Apps and Sessions → Manage app permissions
  • YouTube: Google Account Permissions
