Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 20, 2026 (Updated: October 27, 2026)
Core Principle: Shortsyt Studio is a 100% desktop application that processes and renders video strictly on your local machine. We do not operate external tracking servers and we never collect, store, or sell your personal data.

1. Overview

Shortsyt Studio is an autonomous desktop video creation tool designed for gaming content creators. This Privacy Policy details how data is accessed, handled, and protected when using the desktop application and its integrations.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

Shortsyt does not collect personal information. Specifically:

  • No user account credentials or passwords are sent to external servers.
  • No tracking cookies, pixels, or third-party behavioral analytics are embedded in the app.
  • No personal data or video files are sold, rented, or distributed to any third party.

3. TikTok Content Posting API Integration

When you connect your TikTok account to Shortsyt Studio via OAuth 2.0 PKCE:

  • Local Token Storage: Access tokens and refresh tokens are stored exclusively on your local computer in your local project workspace. They are never transmitted to any third-party or developer-owned backend.
  • Requested Scopes: We request only video.publish, video.upload (to upload video chunks on your behalf), and user.info.basic (to verify your username/avatar in the local desktop UI).
  • Immediate Revocation: You can disconnect and revoke access at any time via TikTok mobile app > Settings & Privacy > Security > Apps and Sessions > Manage app permissions.

4. Video Processing & Media Files

All video analysis (OCR, kill-feed detection) and FFmpeg 9:16 rendering happen locally on your PC. Rendered video files are uploaded directly from your machine to TikTok / YouTube / Meta servers over encrypted HTTPS connections.

5. Compliance with Platform Guidelines

Shortsyt operates in full accordance with the TikTok Terms of Service and TikTok Community Guidelines.
