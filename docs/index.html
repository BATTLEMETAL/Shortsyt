TIKTOK CONTENT POSTING API v2

Direct Post & Creator Integration

Shortsyt Studio connects directly to the official TikTok Developer Platform to allow gaming creators to publish their rendered highlight clips straight to their TikTok profile.

We request only the minimum required OAuth scopes needed for publishing your own video content:

  • video.publish Initiates and publishes video posts on TikTok
  • video.upload Uploads video chunk payloads securely via HTTPS
  • user.info.basic Displays your TikTok profile name & avatar in the dashboard

Security & Compliance Commitment

Shortsyt complies fully with the TikTok Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

• OAuth tokens are encrypted locally on the user's computer.
• The user can revoke permissions at any time via TikTok Settings > Security > Manage App Permissions.
• No user telemetry, credentials, or tracking cookies are collected.

How Shortsyt Works

From local match recording to viral multi-platform short in 3 steps.

01

Select & Scan Clip

Drop gameplay MP4s recorded by Outplayed, Medal, or OBS. The OCR detector finds kill feeds, champion names, and high-impact timestamps automatically.

02

Render & Polish 9:16

The FFmpeg engine crops to vertical 9:16, applies dynamic camera pans, synchronizes kill drops to music beats, generates custom thumbnails, and runs automated QA checks.

03

Publish to TikTok & Socials

Simultaneously publish or schedule to TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram Reels with matching tags, descriptions, and automatic thumbnails.
