Shortsyt Studio is an autonomous desktop video automation tool that identifies highlight moments in gameplay video recordings, renders vertical 9:16 short-form video clips with visual effects, and publishes them to authorized social media platforms (TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Meta Reels) on behalf of the account owner.
