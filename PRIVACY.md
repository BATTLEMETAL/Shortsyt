# Privacy Policy — Shortsyt

**Effective date:** 2026-10-20

## 1. Overview
Shortsyt is a personal automation tool for publishing gaming highlight clips. This policy explains what data is handled and how.

## 2. Data We Collect
Shortsyt does **not** collect any personal data. Specifically:
- No user account data is stored on external servers
- No analytics or tracking is performed
- No data is sold or shared with third parties

## 3. OAuth Tokens
When you authorize Shortsyt to access your TikTok or YouTube account:
- OAuth tokens are stored **locally** on your device only (in `accounts/` directory)
- Tokens are used solely to upload videos on your behalf
- Tokens can be revoked at any time via the respective platform's security settings

## 4. Video Content
- Gameplay footage is processed locally on your device
- Rendered clips are uploaded directly to your authorized platform accounts
- No video content is stored or processed on external servers

## 5. TikTok API Data
When using TikTok Content Posting API:
- Only `video.publish` scope is requested
- Only `open_id` (anonymous identifier) is stored locally
- No profile data, followers, or private information is accessed

## 6. Your Rights
You may revoke access at any time:
- **TikTok:** Settings → Security → Manage app permissions
- **YouTube:** [Google Account Permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions)
